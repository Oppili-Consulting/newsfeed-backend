newsfeed — Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026 · Applies to the newsfeed Android app and to this website.

newsfeed shows headlines, short snippets and links published by news outlets around the world. You choose which countries, languages and outlets appear. This page explains what the app does and does not do with information about you.

Short version

  • The app has no user accounts and no sign-in.
  • Your choices (countries, languages, outlets, topic tabs, background colour, ad-free status) are stored only on your device.
  • The app contains advertising supplied by Google AdMob. Google may use your advertising ID to select and measure ads, subject to the consent choices you make.
  • If you buy the one-time ad-free product, payment is handled by Google Play. We never see your card details.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

What the app stores on your device

DataWhyWhere
Selected countries, followed outlets, topic tabs, background colour, ad-free flag So the app remembers your feedPrivate app storage (Android DataStore)
Recent headlines and thumbnails for the countries you selected So you can read your feed offlinePrivate app storage (cache files)
The public outlet directory So the app knows which outlets existPrivate app storage

Deleting the app, or using Settings → Clear saved posts, removes this data. None of it is sent to us; we operate no user database.

What the app sends over the internet

  • Requests for public JSON files that contain the outlet directory and recent headlines.
  • Requests for news images when a post has artwork.
  • When you tap a story, your browser (or the in-app Custom Tab) opens the outlet's own website, and that outlet's privacy policy applies from then on.
  • Advertising and consent requests described below.

Advertising and consent

The free version of the app shows ads through Google AdMob. Before any ad request is made, the app asks for consent using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) where consent is required — for example in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. You can change your choices at any time in Settings → Privacy options.

Depending on your choices, Google and its partners may process your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

If you decline personalised advertising, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised. Buying the one-time ad-free product removes advertising from the app.

Purchases

The one-time “ad-free” purchase is a non-consumable Google Play in-app product. Google processes the payment and tells the app whether the product is owned; we do not receive your name, address or payment details. Google's handling of that transaction is governed by the Google privacy policy and Google Play's terms.

News content and third parties

newsfeed does not host or republish full articles. It shows a headline, a short snippet and a link, and names the outlet it came from. All content belongs to its publisher and opens on the publisher's own website. Each publisher has its own privacy policy and terms.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Because the app keeps no server-side account or profile, most rights (access, correction, deletion) are satisfied by the fact that your data lives on your own device and can be cleared by you at any time. For questions about advertising data, use the Google privacy controls linked above, or contact us and we will help where we can.

Security

Data is transferred over HTTPS. App preferences and caches live in the app's private storage area, which other apps cannot read on a normal (non-rooted) device.
